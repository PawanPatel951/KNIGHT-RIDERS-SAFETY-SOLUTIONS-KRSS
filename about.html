About KRSS

A people-first approach to protection.

Knight Riders Safety Solutions (KRSS) is a forerunner in the region, known for its trained, committed, motivated and disciplined workforce.

We deliver high-quality services to large corporate houses and commercial complexes in metro and tier-2 / tier-3 cities — across security services, facility management and fire fighting.

  • Industrial protection & security solutions
  • Leadership drawn from retired police and para-military officers
  • Operational & approved by multi-state governments
